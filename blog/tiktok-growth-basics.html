TikTok Growth Basics: What to Fix First

If a video is not getting the response you expected, avoid changing everything at once.

1. Fix the hook

The first seconds should quickly tell the viewer why the video is worth watching.

2. Make the topic clear

Use one clear idea. A focused video is easier for viewers to understand and for search systems to categorize.

3. Add a useful CTA

Ask for one action: comment, save, follow or share. Match the CTA to the video's purpose.

4. Test, don't guess

Compare several posts and use your own audience data. There is no universal magic formula.
