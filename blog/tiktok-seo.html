TikTok SEO: Keywords That Actually Help

TikTok search optimization starts with making the topic understandable.

Use one primary phrase

Choose the phrase your target viewer would naturally search for and use it where it fits.

Support it with context

Captions, spoken words and on-screen text can reinforce the topic.

Don't stuff keywords

Write for humans first. A readable caption is more useful than a pile of repeated phrases.
